# Problem Statement — Case Study 10

For five days, record the quantity of food prepared and quantity left over. Calculate total food prepared, total leftover, and waste percentage. Compare waste percentage with a demonstration target of 10%.

## Objectives
1. Store daily quantities in lists.
2. Validate prepared and leftover quantities.
3. Use loops and arithmetic to calculate totals and percentage.
4. Compare waste percentage with a target using a condition.

## Assumptions
- The program records five days.
- Quantities are non-negative integer units; prepared quantity must be greater than zero each day.
- Leftover quantity cannot exceed prepared quantity.
- The 10% waste target is illustrative for this classroom exercise, not an official standard.
