# Algorithm — Case Study 10: Food Waste Monitoring System

1. Read prepared and leftover quantities for five days.
2. Validate that prepared is positive and leftover is between zero and prepared.
3. Store valid quantities in separate lists.
4. If any entry is invalid, display an error and skip summary calculations.
5. Calculate total prepared and total leftover.
6. Calculate waste percentage as total leftover divided by total prepared times 100.
7. Compare the percentage with the 10% illustrative target and display the result.
